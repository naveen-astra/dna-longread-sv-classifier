SV Triage Bench

Sniffles2 and cuteSV propose structural-variant candidates from Oxford Nanopore reads. A calibrated Random Forest scores each one as real or artefact. Every candidate below is from the held-out chr22 test windows, which the model never saw in training or tuning, and is checked against the GIAB v5.0q truth set.

Inspect a candidate

Pick any of the 281 test candidates to see the locus, the model's decision, what GIAB says, and which evidence moved the score.

Move the decision threshold

The model outputs a probability. The threshold decides which candidates are kept. 0.39 was chosen on the chr19 validation windows; dragging it here shows the precision–recall trade on chr22 and updates the decisions above.

Tier-1 pilot: SV-rich windows on chr16 (train), chr19 (validation) and chr22 (test), so the numbers are not genome-representative. SHAP values explain the forest's raw vote before calibration. "Artefact" means a candidate inside a GIAB confident region with no truth match.
